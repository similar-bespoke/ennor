## General Description & Layout

### Hull

- Semi-displacement hull form designed by Tony Castro with a fine entry bow, good load carrying sections and a relatively flat run to the stern.
- Hand laid E-glass and multi-axial fabrics with Crystic Crestomer resin.
- Hull stiffened with foam sandwich longitudinal stringers and transverse ring frames.
- Engine beds are integral with the hull moulding.
- Hull moulding in colour (TBC) with boot line (TBC).
- Six fixed portlights providing light to the accommodation.
- Hull below waterline: International Gelshield 200 epoxy primer (3 coat) + underwater epoxy coating (2 coat) + Micron Extra antifouling (2 coat). **[Option confirmed]**
- Hull above waterline finished in Awlgrip polyurethane paint system.

### Deck & Superstructure

- Deck and superstructure moulded in standard white, sandwich construction with end grain balsa core.
- Bonded to the hull with Plexus structural adhesive.
- Finished in Awlgrip polyurethane paint with non-slip areas in Awlgrip Griptex.
- Synthetic teak decking — full deck to match aft cockpit. **[Option confirmed]**
- Foredeck has an opening hatch for access to the anchor locker.
- Coachroof includes large skylight running down the centre with removable sun lounger cushions either side.
- Sidedecks in synthetic teak (matching cockpit).
- Superstructure has curved front windscreen with pantograph wipers. Side windscreens run aft with opening sections for ventilation and a door for access to/from the helm position.
- Mast on top of superstructure for nav lights, anchor light and radar.
- Electric sliding sunroof. **[Option confirmed]**

### Exterior Cockpit

- Spacious exterior cockpit laid in cork/synthetic teak.
- Seating at aft end with access through centre to bathing platform.
- Forward area has wet bar with fridge and storage (starboard), lounge area (port).
- Provision for storing fenders, mooring lines and liferaft.
- Aft bathing platform with stainless swim ladder and folding cleats for dinghy.
- Tender chocks on platform. **[Option confirmed]**
- Hot and cold deck shower near stern. **[Option confirmed]**

### Anchor Locker

- Self-draining anchor locker at bow, accessed from deck.
- 21kg Ultra Anchor with stainless steel swivel. **[Upgraded from std 20kg Delta]**
- 40m of stainless steel chain. **[Upgraded from std galvanised]**
- Electric anchor windlass.
- Anchor chain counter (Lewmar AA150). **[Option confirmed]**
- Fresh water anchor wash in bosun's locker. **[Option confirmed]**

### Exterior Stainless & Equipment

- Stainless pulpit running back to exterior cockpit with solid top rail.
- Stainless bow roller integrated into bow stem.
- Six stainless cleats and fairleads (bow, midships and stern).
- Stainless fittings for fillers: 2× deck filler for fuel, 1× deck filler for water, 1× black tank pump-out.
- Signwrite name and port to stern. **[STD]**

### Interior Cockpit

- Exterior cockpit leads to interior via sliding doors.
- Wet bar adjacent to interior galley providing natural extension of the cooking area. Second front-opening fridge in wet bar.
- Interior galley (starboard): induction hob, electric oven, sink, front-opening fridge (~50 litres), bin, drawer units and cupboard space. **[Induction hob & electric oven upgraded from gas — option confirmed]**
- U-shaped saloon area (port) with table.
- Saloon seating conversion to double berth. **[Option confirmed]**
- Helm position forward of galley on starboard side: wheel, throttle controls, engine gauges, anchor control and navigational instruments.

### Cabins

- Large owner's cabin forward with double berth accessible from both sides. Drawers to base of berth. Port and starboard hanging lockers plus cupboards and shelving.
- Guest cabin accessed through door on port side at bottom of companionway steps. Twin berths with stowage beneath, large hanging locker and further stowage outboard.
- Sprung mattress to main forward cabin. **[Option confirmed]**
- Carpet to lower deck/cabins. **[Option confirmed]**

### Heads & Shower Compartment

- Electric toilet, wash basin and separate shower area.
- Corian (or similar) surface with mixer tap and mirror.
- Additional lockers outboard for toiletries.
- Separate thermostatic shower control with shower head.
- Composite mouldings and laminate bulkheads with wood trim around locker doors.

### Joinery & Flooring

- Joinery in palest possible wood — hand crafted by cabinet makers. **[Specified light finish]**
- Highlight joinery details in darkest possible wood for helm surround, ceiling strips etc. **[Two-tone detail confirmed]**
- Mixture of solid oak and marine grade veneered oak panels. Locker doors with solid oak edges, each sunk back into panel.
- Matt satin varnish finish.
- Floors in accommodation/interior cockpit: veneered marine plywood (sample to be provided for main deck flooring).
- Light beige fabrics for interiors (all fabric samples to be supplied). **[Option confirmed]**

### Upholstery

- All seating upholstered in customer's choice of fabric (light beige specified).
- Quality composite fire retardant foam throughout.

### General Material Details

- All areas below the waterline flow coated white.
- Areas above the waterline carpeted and/or panelled. Panels covered in fabric.

## Mechanical & Electrical Systems

### Engine

Twin Yanmar 8LV370 370hp engines with VC20 electronic controls and YD42 instrument displays at helm position.

### Stern Gear

- Shaft drive through stern tube and P bracket.
- Sureseal fitted to stern tube with flexible hose.
- Stern glands cooled from engine cooling system.
- Four bladed props.
- Through hull mounted rudders.
- S16 QuicKutters rope cutters to shafts. **[Option confirmed]**
- Propspeed to stern gear and propellers. **[Option confirmed — RevC £1,693]**

### Steering System

Helm position on starboard side of interior cockpit. Hydraulic system with greasers fitted to rudder tubes.

### Exhaust & Cooling System

- Wet exhaust system. Cooling water exits below the waterline.
- Sea water cooling for engine passes through strainers near inlet.

### Fuel System

- Stainless steel tanks, holding approximately 1,200 litres (265 gallons).
- Tank monitor senders providing accurate data via NMEA2000 network.
- Primary filter/water separator in addition to engine fuel filter.
- Full fuel polishing system (twin filters). **[Option confirmed]**
- Sight glasses for both fuel tanks. **[Option confirmed]**

### Seacocks

Non-metallic skin fittings for all through hull openings.

### Bilge System

- Automatic bilge pump with switch to main panel (Off/On/Auto).
- Two manual Whale Compaq 50 bilge pumps, operated from interior and exterior cockpit.
- 2× additional high capacity manual bilge pumps. **[Option confirmed]**

### Fresh Water System

- Stainless steel tanks, holding approximately 300 litres (66 gallons).
- High capacity Flowjet pressure pump.
- Hot water via 40 litre calorifier heated by 220V 0.8kW immersion heater element or engine heat exchanger. Thermostat controlled.

### Black Water System

Toilet pumps into black tank. Can be emptied to sea or to shore facility via deck outlet. Black tank vented via no-smells filter overboard.

### Grey Water System

Whale Gulper IC pump via two-way Whale IC manifold with electronic sensor. Full grey water system available as option.

### Refrigeration

- Galley: front-opening fridge (~50 litres).
- Wet bar: additional front-opening fridge.
- 2× fridges total. **[Option confirmed]**
- Additional freezer. **[Option confirmed]**

### Gas System

*Replaced by induction hob and electric oven — no gas system fitted.* **[Option confirmed]**

### Heating

- Webasto Evo Air Top heating system. **[Option confirmed]**

### DC Electrical System

- 12V DC system.
- CZone digital switching and monitoring. **[Option confirmed]**
- Mastervolt Lithium battery system — Electrical Top Pack Plus: 4× 12/6000 Lithium service batteries (1,840Ah at 12V). **[Option confirmed]**
- 2× 12/130Ah AGM engine start batteries (one per engine).
- 25A charger for engine bank.
- 2× Combi Charger/Inverter 12/3000-150 for service bank charging.
- 32A shore power socket with 15m lead.
- Charging via 180A (12V) alternators or shore power (12/75A charger service, 12/25A engine bank).
- LED downlighters and strip lighting throughout. Reading lights to cabins. Red/white lights to heads, red courtesy lights to helm area and companionway steps.
- Engine room lights.
- Port and starboard navigation lights fitted to wheelhouse sides. Steaming, stern and anchor light on mast.
- Solar panels (max output to be determined). **[Option confirmed]**

### AC Electrical System

- 230V AC distributed throughout from direct shore power connection via 1× 32A socket.
- 230V Vimar sockets throughout.
- Separate 230V circuit for calorifier immersion heater.

### Generator

- Fischer Panda 8000iPMS 6.4kW generator. **[Option confirmed]**

### Thrusters & Stabilisation

- Sleipner SE100 proportional bow thruster. **[Option confirmed — note: SE80 was in early wiring diagrams, SE100 recommended by Sleipner]**
- Sleipner SE100 proportional stern thruster. **[Option confirmed]**
- Autoglide trim tabs with NMEA integration. **[Option confirmed]**

### Electronics

- 2× Simrad NSS MFDs (supporting forward-facing sonar — NSX compatibility to be confirmed). **[Held for final tech spec 2024]**
- Simrad HALO 20+ Radar. **[Held for final tech spec 2024]**
- Simrad autopilot. **[Held for final tech spec 2024]**
- VHF with AIS B+ and NMEA integration. **[Held for final tech spec 2024]**
- Echopilot forward scan transducer FLS3D5S. **[Held for final tech spec 2024 — development status concerns noted]**
- Iris 740 camera mounted on bow beneath anchor. **[Held for final tech spec 2024]**
- Marinco S/S 5" searchlight. **[Held for final tech spec 2024]**
- Lumishore white flood light 20". **[Held for final tech spec 2024]**
- Night-vision camera (model TBC). **[Held for final tech spec 2024]**

## Commissioning

Yachts are delivered commissioned and afloat in Falmouth, UK.

On completion at the builder's premises the boat is launched followed by ~1 week for sea trials and systems commissioning. Completed craft handed over after a 1-day handover period. Owner signs handover note and final stage payment becomes due.
